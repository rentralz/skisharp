## Variant: Fast lane categories

### Design stance
Optimize for returning or high-intent users who want to jump straight into a category or retailer path.

### Key choices
- Layout: split hero with interactive chip-based finder
- Typography: still branded, but slightly more utility-first and compressed
- Color: familiar warm neutrals balanced with a darker action panel
- Interaction: chips simulate filtering and self-segmentation above the fold

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: speed, intent-based navigation, future fit if filtering becomes real product functionality
- Weak at: less persuasive for cold organic traffic because it proves value after, not before, the interactive fast lane

### Best for
- Returning visitors
- Users who already know they want jackets, boots, goggles, or skis
- A future-state deals page with richer live filters and analytics-backed personalization
