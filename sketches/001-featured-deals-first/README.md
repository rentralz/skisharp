## Variant: Featured deals first

### Design stance
Lead with credibility and strongest current opportunities before asking the user to browse affiliate categories.

### Key choices
- Layout: editorial hero with proof strip, then featured deals, retailer shortcuts, and category grid
- Typography: large headline and proof-led scan pattern similar to the stronger TurnLab homepage sections
- Color: warm neutrals and dark CTA to fit the existing brand
- Interaction: primary CTA jumps to top deals, secondary CTA jumps to categories

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: trust-building, click motivation, marketing clarity, better first impression for new organic visitors
- Weak at: not the fastest layout for users who already know they only want one category and do not care about curation context

### Best for
- Search visitors landing on /deals
- First-time users skeptical of affiliate-heavy pages
- Users comparing multiple retailers before clicking out
