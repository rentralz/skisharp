Parallel Turn Fundamentals

🔵 Level 4 — Intermediate Groomed Runs Turning
"Learn how to keep both skis parallel through the entire turn — the skill that separates beginners from real skiers."

⏱ Key Timestamps

  • 0:42
    Stance setup — Notice how the knees flex forward, not sideways. Weight centered over both feet.
  • 1:35
    Turn initiation — Watch the subtle ankle roll that starts the edge change. No upper body rotation.
  • 2:18
    Fall line commitment — Both skis pointed downhill simultaneously. This is the scary-but-essential moment.
  • 3:45
    Common error demo — See how the inside ski leads? That's a stem, not a parallel turn.

✦ What You Should Feel

  • Both skis turning as one unit — like they're connected by an invisible bar
  • Pressure building under the ball of your outside foot through the turn
  • Your upper body staying quiet and facing downhill while legs do the work
  • A moment of weightlessness between turns as you release the edges

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ A-Frame (knees together, feet apart)
Happens when you rotate instead of tipping. Fix: Practice edging drills on gentle terrain.
❌ Inside ski leads the turn
Sign of an incomplete parallel. Fix: Focus on outside ski pressure. Inside ski follows.
❌ Upper body rotates with the turn
Kills edge grip. Fix: Hold poles horizontal across your body while turning.
